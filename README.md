# Sample-Superstore-Sales-Analysis-Dashboard
sample superstore sales analysis dashboard using power bi desktop
**📈 Superstore Sales & Profitability Dashboard**
_Project Overview_
This repository contains the source data (Sample - Superstore.xlsm - Orders.csv) and the final Power BI report (SAMPLE-SUPERSTORE_dashboard.pbix) detailing the sales and operational performance of a global superstore chain.
The objective of this project was to transform raw transactional data into a dynamic, interactive dashboard that allows stakeholders to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify profitable market segments, and quickly diagnose areas of financial loss or operational inefficiency.

**Key Features & Analysis**
The dashboard is designed for high-level strategic review as well as deep-dive operational analysis, structured around the following key insights:
_Financial KPIs:_ Snapshot cards displaying total Sales, overall Profit, and the crucial Profit Ratio for immediate performance tracking.
_Trend Analysis:_ A detailed time-series chart showing the historical progression of Sales and Profit, allowing for the identification of seasonality and growth momentum.
_Geographic & Segment Performance:_ Interactive map visualizations and bar charts to break down sales and profit contributions by Region, State, and Customer Segment.
_Profitability Diagnostics:_ Utilizes a Scatter Plot (Profit vs. Sales) to pinpoint high-volume orders that are currently unprofitable, enabling targeted intervention on specific product lines or customer contracts.
_Product Deep Dive:_ Analysis of Category and Sub-Category performance to understand which product groups are the primary drivers of success and which require mitigation strategies.
_Operational Insights:_ Examination of profit and volume based on Shipping Mode to assess the operational costs associated with different fulfillment speeds.
**Technologies Used**
**Source Data:** Sample - Superstore.xlsm - Orders.csv
**Visualization & Analysis:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop
**Modeling Language**: DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) for creating advanced measures like Profit Ratio and Date Dimension tables.
This dashboard serves as a comprehensive example of business intelligence reporting, leveraging Power BI's capabilities for data transformation, relationship modeling, and visual storytelling.
